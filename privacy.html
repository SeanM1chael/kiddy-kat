🐱 Kiddy Kat Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-29 · Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version: Kiddy Kat has no ads and never sells or shares your family's information for marketing. Kids are known online only by a nickname. Their real name and challenge photos stay on their own device, and location is used only to look up the local weather. A parent can delete the whole family's online data at any time from the dashboard.

Who we are

Kiddy Kat is a family app for daily routines, practice and rewards, made by Sean Johnson. Questions or requests about your family's information: [contact email].

Parents are in charge

Only a parent or legal guardian, 18 or older, can create a Kiddy Kat family. When you sign up, you confirm that and agree to this policy for your kids. We record when you agreed and which version of this policy you agreed to.

What's saved online, and what stays on the device

Saved online (on our servers)Stays only on your kid's device
  • Family name, family code and PINs (PINs are stored scrambled, never as typed)
  • Each kid's nickname and age group (kitten, tiger or lion)
  • App progress: task lists and check marks, stars, pets, rewards and requests, the pretend wallet, practice scores, streaks, league points, and short reading summaries kids type
  • Schoolwork photos a kid or parent chooses to send, until a grown-up deletes them
  • Names and limits for Fans (family members a parent adds to send stars)
  • An address for each device that turned on alerts, so alerts can be delivered
  • School leave time, bedtime and the device's time zone, for reminder alerts
  • Your kid's real first name and age
  • Photos taken for challenges and outside time
  • The device's location (used only to show local weather)
  • A parent's phone number, if you add one for texting

How we use it

Only to run the app for your family: syncing progress between your devices, showing your dashboard, sending the alerts you turn on, and running the features you use. We don't build advertising profiles, show ads, or sell or rent information to anyone.

Services that help run Kiddy Kat

  • Supabase stores the online data and runs our server code.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's web pages.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads schoolwork photos and makes practice questions, but only when a grown-up asks it to. We don't send names, schools or teachers with those requests.
  • Apple, Google and other browser alert services deliver the alerts you turn on.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather. The device asks it directly for the weather at its approximate location.

Like most websites, these services may briefly record technical details such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep things secure.

Your choices and rights

  • See it: your dashboard shows what's saved for each kid. Email us for a full copy.
  • Change it: edit lists, rewards and settings in the app at any time.
  • Delete it: on the dashboard, Delete my family permanently removes your family's online data: kids, progress, photos, Fans, alert devices and logins. Information kept only on a device stays there until you remove the app from that device.
  • Stop: turn off alerts on any device, or stop using the app, at any time.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it, or delete your family. Logins expire after 90 days. Alert records and usage counts used for limits are kept only as long as those limits need them.

Security

Every request must carry a valid login. PINs are scrambled with a one-way method. Wrong PINs lock logins for 15 minutes after 5 tries. No system is perfectly secure, so pick PINs that are hard to guess and keep the parent PIN private.

Changes to this policy

If we make a big change to what we collect or how we use it, we'll ask parents to agree again before it applies.
