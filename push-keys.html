🔑 Kiddy Kat alert keys

This page makes the key pair that lets Kiddy Kat send push alerts. The keys are made right here in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Do this once: making new keys later turns off everyone's alerts until they turn them on again.

1. Public key (safe to share)

Goes in two places: config.js (as pushKey) and Supabase secret VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY.

2. Private key (secret!)

Only goes in Supabase secret VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY. Never in code, GitHub or a chat.

When both are saved, close this page. The keys are not kept anywhere else.
